Philosophy

We started with a simple observation: the clothing people love in the field is rarely the clothing they are shown online, and the digital fashion they are shown online rarely connects to anything they can wear. Two wardrobes, one person.

Aethel & Code closes the gap. Every garment is cut for real weather first — wind, wet stone, a long day on your feet — and then modelled with the same fidelity, so the piece you own and the piece you render are genuinely the same object.

The design language sits between the restrained and the technical. A dry wool trouser, a shell with no visible branding, a cap in waxed cotton. Nothing shouts. Everything works.

Materials

We buy from mills and factories we can name, and we keep the list short.

  • Italian wool from a mill in Biella, woven dry and finished without chemical softeners.
  • Recycled ripstop and 3-layer nylon, PFC-free DWR, from a Japanese supplier with traceable feedstock.
  • Waxed British cotton, re-waxable indefinitely, from a mill that has been running since 1868.
  • Extra-fine merino, 17.5 micron, mulesing-free and traceable to the flock.

Production runs are small — 120 to 400 units per style — and we visit the makers each season. When a fabric has to change, we say so on the product page.

The digital twin

Each physical garment is scanned and rebuilt as a 3D asset: patterns intact, seams as stitched, cloth set to behave like the real thing. It ships with your order at no extra cost, delivered by email as a link the moment the order is confirmed.

Digital-only pieces — skins, studies, the season lookbook — can be bought on their own, for avatars, renders, films and editorial work.

Our position on digital fashion A digital garment is not a gimmick or a collectible. It is the same design, usable in a different medium. We price it accordingly and licence it plainly.

The studio

We work from a studio in East London and a second desk in Reykjavík, where most of the terrain studies are shot. Small team, no investors, no seasonal calendar borrowed from anyone else.

If you make things and want to talk, write to us. We answer everything within two working days.
