Shipping

Orders are dispatched from London within one to two working days. Tracked shipping is free over £250; below that it is a flat £8 in the UK and £18 to the EU, US, Canada and Iceland.

Duties and import taxes for destinations outside the UK are calculated at checkout, so nothing is collected at the door. If a parcel is returned to us because duties were refused, we refund the garment and deduct the return shipping.

Returns

Thirty days from delivery, unworn and with tags attached. Start a return by writing to studio@aethelandcode.com with your order number; we will send a prepaid label for UK orders.

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of arrival.
  • Exchanges are sent as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier.
  • Sale pieces and altered garments are final sale.
  • If a garment arrives faulty, we cover everything and repair or replace it.

Digital assets and returns Because files cannot be returned once delivered, digital purchases are final. If a file is faulty, incomplete or does not match its description, we replace it or refund it in full — that promise has no time limit.

Digital delivery

Digital assets are delivered instantly via email after purchase. The message contains a download link and a licence key; both are valid indefinitely.

  • With a garment: the digital twin is included at no extra cost and is emailed when your order is confirmed — before the garment ships.
  • Digital only: bought on its own, the asset is emailed within seconds of payment.
  • Lost the email: write to us with your order number and we resend the link, free, any number of times.
  • File formats: CLO 3D projects, USD, GLB, FBX or OBJ, with 2K–4K PBR textures. The format list on each product page is exact.

Licence

Digital assets are licensed, not sold. The standard licence covers personal, portfolio and editorial use, including social media, lookbooks, films and client presentations where no product is sold.

  • Permitted: render, animate, modify and publish the asset in editorial or portfolio work with credit where practical.
  • Not permitted: resale, redistribution, sublicensing, or inclusion in a competing asset library.
  • Commercial use — advertising, games, virtual try-on — is quoted per project. Ask the studio.
  • No NFTs: the licence does not extend to minting or trading the asset.

Care & repair

Every garment carries care notes on its product page. Beyond that, we repair anything we made, for as long as we are making things: seam repairs and hardware are free, fabric replacement is charged at cost.

Re-waxing for waxed cotton is offered once a year at the studio, or by post for the cost of return shipping.

Contact

Any question about the above — or anything not covered here — reaches a person at studio@aethelandcode.com. Full studio details are on the contact page.
